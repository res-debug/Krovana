Stephen Ochanda
Founder & Managing Director, Krovana

Stephen Ochanda is the Founder and Managing Director of Krovana, an African strategic advisory and responsible AI firm focused on helping ambitious organizations move from potential to execution.

His work brings together strategy, finance, research, data and stakeholder engagement, with a focus on investment readiness, business growth and responsible innovation.

Through Krovana, Stephen works at the intersection of capital, organizational capability and emerging technology — helping businesses and organizations prepare for what comes next.
