Krovana's work is aligned with five priority UN Sustainable Development Goals. We contribute through the practical work we do with organizations across Africa.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Through Krovana AI+, we build AI literacy and organizational capabilities that help individuals, teams and leaders understand and use emerging technologies effectively and responsibly.

AI Literacy Workforce Capability Digital Skills Responsible Technology Education Leadership Development
SDG 8

Decent Work and Economic Growth

Krovana helps ambitious African businesses strengthen their foundations, prepare for investment, access growth opportunities and build sustainable enterprises capable of creating economic value.

Investment Readiness Business Growth Entrepreneurship Capital Access Organizational Capability Sustainable Enterprise Development
SDG 9

Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

Krovana supports African organizations in strengthening innovation capabilities, adopting emerging technologies responsibly and building the systems required for sustainable growth.

Innovation Responsible AI Technology Adoption Business Infrastructure Investment Readiness Strategic Growth

Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Through responsible AI and governance-focused advisory work, Krovana helps organizations think more deliberately about accountability, risk, transparency and institutional resilience.

AI Governance Responsible Technology Organizational Governance Risk Management Accountability Transparency
